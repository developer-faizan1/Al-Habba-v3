About Al Habbai

Building Trust,
Delivering Excellence

Al Habbai Contracting, established in Dubai in 1993 by the Al Habbai Family, is a leading contractor known for integrity, teamwork, quality, and commitment. Led by CEO Mr. Ali Al Habbai, the company has delivered prestigious projects across Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, Media City, Palm Jumeirah, and other prominent destinations.
